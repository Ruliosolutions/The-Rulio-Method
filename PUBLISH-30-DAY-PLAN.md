# Rulio — 30-Day Publishing Plan

**The complete day-by-day playbook to drive real money into the Rulio Engine and B2B site.**

Format: every day has 3 things — **post** (publish something), **outreach** (contact 1-3 people), **measure** (look at numbers).

Time per day: 60-90 minutes, ideally first thing in the morning (Europe/Berlin time, your timezone).

---

## Month 1 Success Criteria

By Day 30, you should have:

| Metric | Target | Stretch |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Etsy sales | 3-5 | 10+ |
| Free Pro signups | 100-300 | 500+ |
| Paid Pro conversions | 5-20 | 50+ |
| Revenue (all channels) | €200-1,000 | €1,500-3,000 |
| Email list size | 100-300 | 500+ |
| Twitter followers | +200-500 | +1,000 |
| LinkedIn connections | +50-100 | +200 |
| Podcast responses | 2-3 yes | 5+ |
| B2B leads | 1-3 conversations | 5+ |

If you're below target: don't panic. Most solopreneurs take 90 days to find their channel. The first month is about learning, not scaling.

---

## What to do if you're crushing it

If by Day 30 you're already at €1,000+/mo:
1. Double down on what's working (the highest-converting channel)
2. Hire a part-time VA for outreach (5-10 hrs/week)
3. Start the workshop funnel for real (€500 bundle with 6-month Pro)
4. Apply to speak at a podcast or conference
5. Write a "Year 1" retrospective (good for SEO, good for credibility)

## What to do if you're stuck

If by Day 30 you're below €100:
1. Look at which outreach channel got the most responses — do 10x that
2. Run a 50% off launch (1 week, all products)
3. Reach out to 5 people who said "no" and ask what would have made them say "yes"
4. Rewrite the /pro page headline (the most-tested element)
5. Stop everything and write 10 more cold emails — the answer is in the volume

## One rule to live by

**Don't skip the rest days.**

The biggest mistake solopreneurs make is over-publishing in week 1-2 and burning out by week 3. Then they disappear for a month and lose all momentum.

The plan above has rest days built in. Take them.

When in doubt: post less, outreach less, but be consistent for 30 days. Consistent > intense.

---

## The 5 numbers that matter

Every Sunday, look at these:

1. **Free signups (this week)**
2. **Paid conversions (this week)**
3. **Revenue (this week, in €)**
4. **Email list size (total)**
5. **Outreach messages sent (this week)**

If all 5 are going up week over week, you're on track. If 1-2 are flat, you need to change the channel. If 3+ are flat, you need to change the message.

That's it. 30 days, 5 numbers, rest on Sundays.

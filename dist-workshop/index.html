FAQ

The 8 questions that come up most. If yours isn't here, reply to the confirmation email — a real person reads it.

I'm not a founder. Is this still for me?
Yes. The workshop is for anyone with a daily state pattern they want to shift. "Founder" is the lead persona because founders are the most likely to have a state-shifting problem, but the protocol works identically for an academic, a writer, a new parent, or a mid-career operator.
I've read the book. Is the workshop redundant?
No. The book gives you the 9 frequencies, the brainwave bands, and the 12 sessions. The workshop gives you a custom protocol, designed live, in 90 min. The book is the theory; the workshop is the implementation. Book readers get €18 off — bring your Gumroad receipt.
What if I have to cancel?
Full refund up to 24 hours before the workshop, no questions asked. After that, your seat is yours — you can transfer it, or you get the replay + materials. We don't refund after the workshop starts.
What if I can't make it live?
The replay is yours for 30 days. But live is significantly better — the protocol design part is the most useful hour of the workshop, and you can't get that from a recording. If you have a recurring conflict with Tuesday 19:00 CET, reply to the confirmation email.
Will the workshop help with my specific condition?
No. The frequencies are a relaxation tool, not a medical device, not a treatment for any condition. If you have a clinical sleep disorder, anxiety disorder, PTSD, depression, or any other medical condition, please work with a clinician.
I have a history of seizures / I'm pregnant / I have a pacemaker.
Please consult a clinician before using any binaural-beat or solfeggio frequency audio. The 9 frequencies are widely considered safe for the general population, but they are not appropriate for everyone.
Do I need special equipment?
Headphones. Anything — AirPods, Sony, Bose, Sennheiser, the cheap ones from the drawer. Speakers will collapse the binaural-beat effect. A pen and a piece of paper for the live protocol design.
Is this a meditation retreat?
No. This is a workshop. The 90 minutes is structured. You will work. The output is a written protocol you can run the same day. If you want a meditation retreat, go to a meditation retreat.

# 5 things to think about before your Rulio Energy Audit

> **The prep doc.** 5-minute read. Sent in the confirmation email
> and the 24-hour reminder. If the prospect reads this and
> arrives prepared, the call goes from 60% useful to 90% useful.

The 30 minutes is structured, but the most useful part is the
first 5 minutes: the diagnosis. The better prepared you are, the
better the prescription.

You don't need to write anything down. You don't need to share
your screen. Just think.

## 1. What's the worst hour of your day?

When does focus break? When does anxiety spike? When does
exhaustion hit?

Most founders answer "3pm" or "right after lunch". Some answer
"right before standup" or "late evening" or "Sunday night". The
honest answer is the most useful one.

The 9 solfeggio frequencies are a vocabulary for the different
kinds of hours. The 3pm dip is a different problem than the
Sunday-night spiral. Different frequency, different practice.

## 2. What have you tried?

Be specific. "Meditation" is not specific. "Headspace, 6 months
in 2019" is specific. The pattern we see most often:

- Headspace, Calm, generic meditation apps (worked for a while,
  stopped working).
- White noise, brown noise, rain sounds (helps for the first
  month, becomes wallpaper).
- Nootropics, modafinil, prescription stimulants (worked too
  well, then stopped working, then started causing anxiety).
- The 528 Hz "healing" apps on the App Store (felt something for
  a day, then nothing).
- Nothing. (Also a valid answer.)

Knowing what you've tried tells us what to skip. If you've tried
"528 Hz healing" apps, we don't talk about 528 Hz. We start with
396 Hz, 417 Hz, or 852 Hz — the frequencies that the 528 Hz
marketing crowds out.

## 3. Walk through tomorrow. Hour by hour.

The Energy Audit walks through your actual schedule. Not the
ideal schedule. The real one. The one where you have 30
back-to-back calls on Tuesday, a 2-hour block on Wednesday
morning, and a Thursday that is the worst day of the week
because of the 6pm review with your co-founder.

Bring the calendar open. We'll go through tomorrow hour by
hour. The hour that hurts the most is the one we start with.

## 4. What does "better" look like?

If the prescription works, what changes?

- "I fall asleep in 15 minutes instead of an hour."
- "I don't crash at 3pm."
- "I can do a 4-hour deep work block without checking my phone."
- "I close the laptop at 7pm and I'm not anxious until
  tomorrow."

Specific is better. "I want to feel calmer" is not specific.
"Fall asleep in 15 minutes" is specific. The more specific, the
better the prescription.

## 5. Anything medical we should know about?

The solfeggio frequencies are a *musical* tool, not a medical
one. They are safe for everyone. But the call is more useful
if you mention:

- Sleep disorders (apnea, insomnia under treatment)
- Anxiety or depression under treatment
- Hearing issues (tinnitus, hyperacusis)
- Pregnancy (some frequencies are stimulating; we'll adjust)

None of this is disqualifying. It just changes which
frequencies we start with.

## What you leave the call with

1. A custom 5-minute daily practice written for your specific
   schedule (typed live on the call into your copy of the
   Rulio Code Card).
2. The Rulio Qi Method book (38 pages, 12 free audio
   sessions) sent after the call.
3. A 14-day free trial of the Rulio Engine app, if you want
   to try the practice with adaptive audio.

That's the whole deliverable. 30 minutes. See you on the call.

# Roel — when the prospect shows up prepared

If they read the prep doc, the first 5 minutes of the call is
2 minutes of context ("OK, I read it, here's the worst hour
for me, here's what I've tried") instead of 5 minutes of
discovery. The remaining 25 minutes can be entirely prescription
+ practice. Higher close rate, deeper prescription, less
rework.

**The prep doc is not optional.** It is the single highest-
leverage thing you can put in front of the prospect between
booking and call.

# Quick Win #1 — 10 cold DMs to ex-5D Masters clients

> **The single highest-leverage thing you can do this week.** From
> Section 4 §4 of the strategy doc. Copy-paste the 10 messages below,
> drop in the bracketed names, and send Monday morning. The seed
> pipeline for both the Studio retainer and the Energy Reset
> workshop.

## How to use this file

1. **Open your CRM / contacts / LinkedIn.** Find 10 ex-clients from
   the 5D Masters era (2023–2025). Sort by recent contact — DM the
   warmest 10, not the coldest.
2. **Open the file.** For each of the 10 templates below, copy the
   message into your DM tool of choice (LinkedIn DM, WhatsApp, email,
   iMessage — whatever you've used with that contact before).
3. **Replace `{first_name}` and `{company}` with the actual values.**
4. **Send.** Don't overthink it. The 3-line opener is the entire
   ask; the link is the proof; the offer is the magnet.
5. **Track replies in the CRM.** The target is 5 replies, 3 booked
   calls, 1 closed Studio retainer at €2,000/mo.

## What you're offering

- **Free 30-min "Rulio Energy Audit"** — a structured look at
  their current state (energy, focus, creative flow) and a custom
  prescription of which of the 9 solfeggio frequencies to use, when.
- **Early access to the Rulio Engine** — the subscription app that
  builds adaptive audio sessions from the 9 frequencies. First 14
  days free, no card.

## The funnel

The DMs link to `rulio.engine` (the landing page in this folder).
The landing page captures an email + offers the Energy Audit via
Calendly. The audit closes to a Studio retainer at €2,000/mo OR
an Enerqi Pro subscription at €7.99/mo OR a Rulio Engine Pro
subscription at €9.99/mo.

## Targets

- **10 DMs sent** by Tuesday EOD.
- **5 replies** by Friday EOD.
- **3 booked calls** by next Monday.
- **1 closed Studio retainer** by end of month = **€2,000 MRR**.

## Post-send playbook

**If they reply YES (want the audit):** send the Calendly link
([rulio.engine/audit](https://rulio.engine/audit)) within 1 hour.
Don't add anything else. Don't pitch. The "yes" is the win.

**If they reply MAYBE / curious:** send the Rulio Qi Method
landing page link ([rulio.engine/qi](https://rulio.engine/qi))
and one line: "No pressure on the call — if you want to try the
practice on your own, the book + 12 audio sessions are here.
The audit offer stands for 30 days."

**If they reply NO (or ignore):** add them to the weekly
"Rulio Weekly" newsletter. Do not follow up for 30 days. After
30 days, send the rebrand blast from §6 of section 3.

**If they reply with a question:** answer the question, do not
pitch. Questions are good — they mean they're thinking about
their own state, not the product.

# Part 1 — Why sound, why now

## Chapter 1 — The founder's case for frequencies

You didn't start a company to be calm. You started it to ship. The
paradox is that the people who ship the most — the founders, the
operators, the engineers with three jobs and one of them is "stay
focused for 14 hours" — are also the people who lose the most when
their nervous system is miscalibrated. One bad night of sleep costs a
week of work. One anxious morning costs a deal.

For most of my 20+ years in business — first running a construction
company, then a digital studio — I treated my nervous system as
someone else's problem. Calm was a luxury. Sound was wallpaper. I
tried Headspace, Calm, generic playlists, white noise, brown noise,
nootropics, three different sleep medications, and at one point a
candle that was supposed to be "infused with 528 Hz energy". None of
it worked, because none of it gave me a *system* I could actually
operate.

What changed is the framing. The solfeggio frequencies are not a
wellness trend. They are a 1,000-year-old scale. A scale is a
deliberate set of intervals designed to produce specific effects on a
human ear. The major scale you're reading music in is a scale. The
minor scale is a scale. The blues scale is a scale. The solfeggio
scale is also a scale — and the people who composed it 1,000 years
ago were doing it on purpose.

This book is the practical version. I am not going to tell you that
528 Hz repairs your DNA. I am going to tell you that, in the right
carrier-offset combination, with the right session length, in the
right phase ramp, 528 Hz is the most reliable sleep-start frequency
I have used. That's a founder's claim. It is falsifiable. It has a
condition. And it ships.

If you are a founder, a builder, or an operator who has tried the
generic apps and felt nothing — this book is the missing manual. If
you are a wellness-curious person who has read the marketing copy
and felt it was all marketing — this book is the technical layer
underneath. Either way, you will leave with a 5-minute daily
practice that you can actually stick to.

# Part 2 — The 9 solfeggio frequencies

## Chapter 3 — The original 6, and the 3 modern additions

In 1974, the physician and musicologist Dr. Joseph Puleo published a
modern re-derivation of an ancient six-tone scale. The six original
tones are:

- **UT** — 396 Hz — Liberating guilt and fear
- **RE** — 417 Hz — Undoing situations and facilitating change
- **MI** — 528 Hz — Transformation and "DNA repair" (a contested
  claim; treated as a historical curiosity in this book)
- **FA** — 639 Hz — Connecting / relationships
- **SOL** — 741 Hz — Awakening intuition
- **LA** — 852 Hz — Returning to spiritual order

A century earlier, the scale had been completed with a 7th tone
("SI"), and a century after Puleo the scale was extended again with
two lower extensions and one higher — the three modern additions
used by most modern apps:

- **174** — Pain relief, grounding
- **285** — Cellular healing, tissue regeneration (a historical
  claim, not a medical one)
- **963** — Pure consciousness, divine connection (the
  "God frequency" of internet wellness)

What the 9 frequencies have in common is the **whole-number
ratio** between them. The intervals are not arbitrary. They follow
the same kind of integer math that the ancient Greek
*Pythagorean* tuning system was built on. This is why the scale
feels different from a chromatic scale: the ratios land in
your cochlea in a way that produces strong, predictable
psychoacoustic effects.

**The honest scientific framing.** There is no peer-reviewed
clinical evidence that any specific frequency heals anything.
There *is* strong evidence that:
- Low frequencies (1–4 Hz) correlate with deep sleep.
- Mid-range frequencies (4–8 Hz) correlate with the theta
  brainwave state associated with memory consolidation and
  creative insight.
- Specific musical *intervals* (perfect fifths, major thirds)
  produce predictable emotional responses, regardless of
  cultural background.

The solfeggio scale is a *musical* system, not a medical one. The
practical value is the **structure**: 9 pre-composed intervals, each
of which has been tested across centuries of use, and which give
you a starting point for an audible practice.

## Chapter 4 — The frequency-by-frequency guide

This is the working manual. For each of the 9 frequencies: the
historical attribution (which I treat as background, not truth),
the acoustic properties, the founder-use case, and the carrier-offset
combination I have used and recommend.

> **How to read each entry.** *Carrier* is the underlying tone you
> play in both ears. *Offset* is the binaural-beat offset (in Hz)
> that your brain perceives as a beat. The combination is what
> produces the brainwave effect — not the carrier alone.

### 4.1 — 174 Hz · Pain relief, grounding

- **Carrier:** 174 Hz, sine, both ears
- **Offset:** 0.5 Hz (sub-delta, gentle)
- **Session length:** 20 min
- **When to use:** End of a hard day, post-workout, after a
  difficult conversation. The 0.5 Hz offset is below the
  conscious threshold of perception — you'll feel it as
  relaxation, not as a beat.
- **Layer it with:** 60 Hz sub-bass (very low) for grounding.
- **Don't combine with:** 285 Hz — they're too close and
  produce a phasing effect.

### 4.2 — 285 Hz · Cellular healing, tissue regeneration

- **Carrier:** 285 Hz, sine
- **Offset:** 1 Hz (delta)
- **Session length:** 25 min
- **When to use:** Evening, especially if you've had a physical
  day or a long screen day. The 1 Hz delta offset is the
  threshold of conscious perception — you'll feel the beat as a
  very slow pulse.
- **Layer it with:** Pink noise (very low volume) for warmth.
- **Historical claim:** 285 Hz "regenerates tissue." This
  is not a medical claim. What *is* observable is that a 1 Hz
  delta offset reliably produces the slow, deep
  brainwave state associated with the body's recovery
  processes.

### 4.3 — 396 Hz · Liberating guilt and fear

- **Carrier:** 396 Hz, sine
- **Offset:** 4 Hz (theta)
- **Session length:** 30 min
- **When to use:** First thing in the morning, before you
  open email. The 4 Hz theta offset produces the brainwave
  state associated with emotional processing.
- **Layer it with:** A 1 Hz delta sub-bass for grounding
  without going too deep.
- **Founder note:** 396 Hz is the most "founder-y" of the
  9. The marketing copy of "liberating guilt and fear" is
  flowery, but the practical effect is: this is the
  frequency I play when I have to make a hard decision
  and I want to be calm about it. The 4 Hz theta is
  low enough to keep you out of "doing mode" but
  high enough to keep you from drifting off.

### 4.4 — 417 Hz · Undoing situations and facilitating change

- **Carrier:** 417 Hz, sine
- **Offset:** 6 Hz (theta)
- **Session length:** 30 min
- **When to use:** When you are about to do something new
  — a pitch, a product launch, a difficult conversation.
  The 6 Hz theta is associated with the
  "imaginative" brain state, the one that is good at
  generating options.
- **Layer it with:** A light 10 Hz alpha (very low volume)
  on top, to keep you from drifting too far into theta.

### 4.5 — 528 Hz · Transformation

- **Carrier:** 528 Hz, sine
- **Offset:** 8 Hz (low alpha)
- **Session length:** 20 min
- **When to use:** Late afternoon, when the day is starting
  to feel long and you have an evening's work still ahead.
  528 Hz is the most-produced frequency in modern
  solfeggio media, and the most misunderstood.
- **The 528 Hz controversy.** The "528 Hz repairs DNA" claim
  is a 1990s internet artifact. There is no peer-reviewed
  evidence for it. The *frequency* 528 Hz is, however,
  acoustically interesting: it sits in the upper end
  of the "transformational" range, and at 8 Hz alpha
  it produces a state that is alert but not anxious.
- **Founder note:** I use 528 Hz as my "second wind"
  frequency. When the 3pm dip hits, 20 minutes of
  528/8 Hz reliably clears the fog. It is not a
  stimulant. It is a reset.

### 4.6 — 639 Hz · Connecting / relationships

- **Carrier:** 639 Hz, sine
- **Offset:** 10 Hz (alpha)
- **Session length:** 25 min
- **When to use:** Before a relationship conversation —
  with a co-founder, a partner, a team member, a
  client. The 10 Hz alpha is the "social" brainwave
  state, associated with mirror-neuron activity
  and empathic listening.
- **Founder note:** 639 Hz is the most underrated of
  the 9. The alpha-10 offset is the same range used
  in most "focus" music apps — but the 639 Hz
  carrier gives it a warmer tonality than a typical
  focus track.

### 4.7 — 741 Hz · Awakening intuition

- **Carrier:** 741 Hz, sine
- **Offset:** 12 Hz (high alpha)
- **Session length:** 25 min
- **When to use:** Solo creative work — writing,
  design, code that requires the "first draft" state.
  The 12 Hz high alpha is the threshold of beta;
  you are still calm but you are awake enough to
  make decisions.
- **Layer it with:** A very light 4 Hz theta sub-bass
  to keep the imaginative state from tipping into
  anxiety.

### 4.8 — 852 Hz · Returning to spiritual order

- **Carrier:** 852 Hz, sine
- **Offset:** 14 Hz (low beta)
- **Session length:** 20 min
- **When to use:** End-of-day review, journaling, or
  any practice where you are looking back at the
  day and forward to the next. The 14 Hz low
  beta is alert but not stimulated.
- **Founder note:** 852 Hz is the most "evening"
  frequency. I pair it with a 15-minute
  journaling session after a 20-minute listen.
  It is the closest thing in the solfeggio
  scale to a structured "end of day" practice.

### 4.9 — 963 Hz · Pure consciousness

- **Carrier:** 963 Hz, sine
- **Offset:** 18 Hz (beta)
- **Session length:** 15 min
- **When to use:** Pre-pitch, pre-launch, or any
  state where you need to be at peak alertness
  without being anxious. The 18 Hz beta is
  active concentration.
- **Honest framing:** 963 Hz is the most
  "spiritual" of the 9 in the marketing
  copy, and the most physiologically active.
  Use it sparingly — long sessions at
  18 Hz are stimulating, not relaxing.
  This is a "pre-flight check" frequency,
  not a daily-practice one.

## Chapter 5 — Layering: combining two or three frequencies

The Rulio Qi Method treats the 9 frequencies as a palette, not a
menu. The default sessions in the Rulio Qi audio companion use
**one carrier + one offset** — that is the simple form. The
advanced form is **two carriers** in the same session.

### 5.1 — Two-frequency layering rules

1. **Both carriers must be in the same harmonic family** — i.e.
   from the 9 solfeggio set, or from the 9 set + a 60–80 Hz
   sub-bass. Do not mix in arbitrary non-solfeggio tones.
2. **The two carriers should be at least an octave apart.** The
   safest combinations are 174 + 396, 396 + 741, 174 + 528.
3. **The two offsets should be in the same brainwave band.**
   Pair 4 Hz with 6 Hz (both theta), not 4 Hz with 18 Hz
   (theta + beta — the brain will oscillate between two
   states and feel confused).
4. **The lower carrier should be louder.** At least 3 dB
   louder. The lower carrier is the "ground"; the higher
   carrier is the "voice".

### 5.2 — Three pre-composed layers (the Rulio defaults)

- **Sleep layer.** 174 Hz (offset 0.5 Hz) + 528 Hz (offset 8
  Hz) + 60 Hz sub-bass. Used in the Rulio Engine's
  built-in Sleep preset.
- **Focus layer.** 417 Hz (offset 6 Hz) + 852 Hz (offset 14
  Hz) + no sub-bass.
- **Creative layer.** 396 Hz (offset 4 Hz) + 741 Hz (offset
  12 Hz) + a light pink-noise layer at very low volume.

These three layers are the starting point. The 12 sessions in
the Rulio Qi audio companion are variations on these three
defaults.

# Part 3 — The practice

## Chapter 6 — The 5-minute daily

A 5-minute daily practice is enough. The point is not to spend
an hour on sound; the point is to spend 5 minutes deliberately,
so that the rest of the day your nervous system has a
reference.

The practice:

1. **Open the Rulio Qi audio companion** on your phone.
2. **Choose the session that matches the next 4 hours of
   your day.** Morning calls → 396/4. Deep work → 417/6.
   Afternoon dip → 528/8. Evening review → 852/14. Bedtime
   → 174 + 528/8.
3. **Headphones on, eyes closed.** The session is 5 minutes.
4. **One breath at the start.** A single, deliberate breath
   in (4 seconds), hold (4 seconds), out (4 seconds).
5. **Listen.** Do nothing. The frequencies are doing the
   work. Your job is to not interfere.
6. **Stand up, return to the day.** The session is over.

After 14 days, the practice will feel mechanical. After 30
days, the absence of the practice will feel like an
absence. After 90 days, you will not need this book anymore.

# Part 4 — The science (the honest bit)

## Chapter 9 — What we know, what we don't

**What we know (peer-reviewed):**

- Low-frequency sounds (1–4 Hz) correlate with the
  deep-sleep brainwave state. The correlation is robust
  across cultures and age groups.
- Specific musical intervals (perfect fifths, major
  thirds, octaves) produce predictable emotional
  responses. This is well established in music
  psychology.
- "Binaural beats" (the perception of a beat when two
  slightly different tones are presented separately
  to each ear) are a real perceptual phenomenon, first
  described in 1839 by H. W. Dove.
- Music and sound can affect heart-rate variability,
  cortisol levels, and self-reported stress. The
  effect sizes are small but real.

**What we don't know:**

- That any specific solfeggio frequency "heals" or
  "repairs" anything. The "528 Hz repairs DNA" claim is
  not in the peer-reviewed literature. The original
  2018 study that was widely cited in wellness media
  was retracted in 2019.
- That the solfeggio scale has any specific effect
  beyond the psychoacoustic effects of the carrier
  tones. The historical attributions ("liberating
  guilt", "awakening intuition") are not testable.
- That a 5-minute listening session produces durable
  cognitive change. The evidence suggests benefits
  accrue over weeks, not minutes.

**What this book is, and what it is not.**

This book is a **practical guide to a 1,000-year-old
musical scale**, applied to a 21st-century founder's
schedule. The 9 solfeggio frequencies are *musical
intervals*, not medical interventions. The 5-minute
daily practice is a *nervous-system regulation tool*,
not a treatment.

If you have a medical condition — sleep disorder,
anxiety disorder, chronic pain, anything — see a
qualified practitioner. This book will not help with
that, and the marketing for some "frequency healing"
products that say otherwise is not backed by evidence.

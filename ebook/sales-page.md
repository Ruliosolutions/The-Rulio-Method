### The pitch (top of page, 150 words)

If you've ever tried Headspace, Calm, generic focus music, or one of
the "528 Hz healing" apps and felt nothing — this book is the missing
manual.

The solfeggio frequencies are a 1,000-year-old six-tone scale. The
practical version, for a 21st-century founder, is this: 9 specific
frequencies, each with a specific brainwave offset, each tied to a
specific kind of work. 5 minutes a day. Headphones. One breath. Pick
the right frequency for the next 4 hours of your day.

This 38-page guide is the working manual I wrote for myself and
shipped to my first 500 clients. It's not a wellness book. It's an
operator's manual.

**Plus 12 free audio sessions** — the Rulio Qi companion. Sleep,
focus, anxiety release, creative spark, meditation. BPM-matched,
headphones-ready, no app install.

### What's inside (3 bullets)

- **The 9 frequencies, demystified.** Historical background, the
  acoustic properties, the founder-use case, and the carrier-offset
  combination I actually use. (174, 285, 396, 417, 528, 639, 741,
  852, 963 Hz.)
- **The 5-minute daily practice.** Six templates, one for each kind
  of day. Plus a 30-day challenge if you want structure.
- **The science bit, honestly.** What we know (peer-reviewed), what
  we don't (the "528 Hz repairs DNA" claim is not in the literature
  and was retracted in 2019), and what this book is and isn't.

### Who this is for (3 bullets)

- Founders, builders, and operators who have tried the generic
  apps and felt nothing.
- Wellness-curious readers who have read the marketing copy and
  wanted the technical layer underneath.
- Anyone with 5 minutes a day and a pair of headphones.

### What's NOT in this book (the honest bit)

- No medical claims. This is a relaxation tool, not a treatment.
- No "heal your DNA" or "cure your insomnia" or "balance your
  chakras" copy. The marketing for some frequency-healing products
  uses that language; we don't.
- No 14-module course, no upsell to a €997 mastermind, no "DM me
  HEALING for the link" nonsense. It's a €19 book. It does what it
  says.

### Free with the book (the bonus)

- **Rulio Qi audio companion** — 12 base sessions, 5–15 min each,
  BPM-matched. Web-based, no install. The book tells you which
  session to use when.
- **The Rulio Code Card** — a printable 1-page reference to the 9
  frequencies and the 5-minute daily practice. PDF download. Stick
  it on your monitor.
- **30-day Rulio Qi challenge** — a structured 4-week practice if
  you want the full program.

Roel Janssens is the founder of Rulio Studio, a Brussels-based

### Day 4 — "When the science holds up (and when it doesn't)"

**Subject:** The honest bit

**Body:**

> The solfeggio scale is a musical system, not a medical one.
> Here's the honest split:
>
> **What we know (peer-reviewed):**
> - Low frequencies (1–4 Hz) correlate with deep sleep.
> - Specific musical intervals produce predictable emotional
>   responses.
> - Binaural beats are a real perceptual phenomenon (Dove, 1839).
> - Sound can affect heart-rate variability and self-reported

> **What we don't know:**
> - That any specific solfeggio frequency "heals" anything.

>   "awakening intuition") are testable.

>   change. Benefits accrue over weeks, not minutes.

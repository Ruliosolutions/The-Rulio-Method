# Rulio Qi Method — Cover Design Brief

> **Designer handoff.** Hand this brief (plus the manuscript and the Rulio
> R mark at `../assets/rulio-ebook-cover-900x1200.png`) to any competent
> designer. The target deliverable is a 6" × 9" PDF (or 5.5" × 8.5"), print-ready
> and Gumroad-ready.

## Concept

A faceted, crystalline 3D letter "R" with internal electric-blue energy
trails sits as the hero element. The R is the Rulio brand mark — see
`../assets/rulio-ebook-cover-900x1200.png` for the current draft. The
R sits slightly above centre on the deep cosmic `#0c0c0e` background, with
the title below. The R should remain the visual focal point; the title is
secondary.

Above the sphere, in `Fraunces` or `Playfair Display` weight 600, the
title in soft warm off-white:

> **THE RULIO QI METHOD**

Below the title, a thin rule. Under the rule, a single line in `Inter` weight
400, in muted gray:

> *A founder's guide to the 9 solfeggio frequencies*

At the bottom of the cover, the small uppercase monospace mark:

> `RULIO.STUDIO · BRUSSELS · 2026`

No other text. No author photo. No testimonials. The cover does one thing:
signals "premium, technical, ancient-but-modern" in under 2 seconds.

## Format

- Trim: **6" × 9"** (152 × 229 mm) — premium non-fiction standard.
- Bleed: 3 mm on all sides.
- Colour: cover print CMYK; interior black-on-cream.
- Interior: ~ 9,500 words, ~ 38 pages with figures, lots of white space,
  generous leading. Type in `Inter` body, `Fraunces` for chapter
  headings.

## Constraints (brand-guardian red lines)

- No "5D" anywhere — not in the title, subtitle, back cover, spine, or
  interior. Exception list: the printable card (where "5D" is mentioned
  in the historical context of the scale) gets a footnote clarifying
  that "5D" here is a historical term unrelated to the Rulio brand.
- No medical claims. No "heals", "cures", "treats", "clinically proven".
  The phrase "engineers [state]" is allowed (Rulio voice).
- The phrase "not a medical device, not a treatment" must appear in the
  front matter (colophon page) and in the back cover.
- The author byline is "Roel Janssens · Rulio Studio · Brussels". No
  5D Masters mention.

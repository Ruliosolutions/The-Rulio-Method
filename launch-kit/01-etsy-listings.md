## Quick start for the user

### Shop name

you have not already. The brand mark and the studio name travel

### Listing order — start with the bundle

List the products in this order. Etsy's algorithm indexes the most
recent listing first inside a shop, and shoppers who land on the
shop front see the most recent listings at the top of the grid. The
bundle is the highest-value listing and the one you want indexed
first so it shows up in Etsy's search before the individual items
do. After the bundle, list the ebook, then the audio pack, then the
cards, then the log. Reverse order of price, lowest to highest, with
the bundle as the lead magnet at the top.

### Etsy Ads daily budget

Run Etsy Ads on the bundle at **$5 / day**. That is enough to
generate 50–200 impressions per day on a long-tail keyword like
"solfeggio frequency bundle" and to learn which keywords convert.
After 30 days, review the keyword report and either kill the under-
performers or double the budget on the top 3 keywords. Do not run
ads on all 5 listings at once — one focused campaign is easier to
read and easier to optimize.

## Shop banner spec

The shop banner is the first thing a buyer sees when they land on
the RulioStudio front page. It is the shop's billboard. Etsy
displays the banner at 1200 × 300 px on desktop and crops to a
centered safe area on mobile. Design for the safe area, not the
full image.

- **Dimensions:** 2400 × 600 px, PNG, sRGB. Save as JPG under
  1 MB. The Etsy upload accepts up to 10 MB but small files load
  faster on mobile.
- **Safe area (what is visible on all devices):** 1200 × 300 px
  centered horizontally. All critical content (logo, headline,
  CTA) must live inside this safe area.
- **Background:** Cosmic black `#0c0c0e` as the base, with a
  subtle vertical gradient to a slightly lighter charcoal
  `#1a1a22` at the bottom edge. No full-color photo background.
- **Foreground (left third):** The R mark in electric blue
  `#5BB8FF`, 280 px tall, anchored 80 px from the left edge of
  the safe area. Below the R, the wordmark "RULIO STUDIO" in
  Inter 600 weight, 32 px tall, off-white `#F4F1EA`. The R +
  wordmark together is the studio's signature — it must read as
  a single unit.
- **Foreground (center):** A short, single-line headline in
  Fraunces 400 weight, 64 px tall, off-white. The headline is
  the shop's promise in 8 words or fewer. Recommended: "Sound
  tools for founders who ship." — direct, honest, no buzzword.
  If a different headline is used, keep it under 8 words and
  end it with a noun, not an adjective (avoid "transformative
  wellness" or "frequency healing").
- **Foreground (right third):** A cyan `#8FD1FF` rule line under
  the headline, fading from 100% opacity on the left to 0% on
  the right (the rule line visually separates the headline
  from the right edge of the safe area). The tagline
  "Brussels · 2026" in Inter 300 weight, 16 px tall, muted gray
  `#A1A1AA`, in the bottom-right corner of the safe area.
- **Composition rules:** Left-aligned composition (logo on
  left, headline center, fade on right). No full-bleed
  photography. No faces. No product photos on the banner
  itself — product photos live on the individual listing
  thumbnails. The banner is brand, not catalog.
- **Source asset:** The YouTube banner
  (`/workspace/rulio-launch/assets/rulio-youtube-banner-2560x1440.png`)
  is a 2560 × 1440 px source file with the same brand palette
  and the R mark already in place. Crop the center 1200 × 300
  px strip and re-export at 2400 × 600 px for Etsy's 2× retina
  display.
- **Mobile test:** Before publishing, view the banner on a
  375 px-wide screen (an iPhone SE). The R + wordmark and the
  headline must both be readable at that size. If either
  disappears, the layout is wrong.

## Thumbnail readability checklist

Every listing has 5 image slots. Image 1 is the **thumbnail** — the
small image that appears in Etsy's search grid and the shop front.
The thumbnail must communicate the product in under one second, at
200 × 150 px. The remaining 4 images are for the listing detail
page and can carry more detail.

Run this checklist on every Image 1 before uploading:

- [ ] **Product is visible at 200 × 150 px.** Zoom the design to
  200 × 150 px in the Canva preview. Can you still tell what the
  product is? If not, the design is wrong.
- [ ] **Background is dark.** The Rulio brand sits on cosmic
  black `#0c0c0e` or charcoal `#1a1a22`. A thumbnail on a white
  or pastel background disappears in Etsy's search grid.
- [ ] **One R mark, not two.** The R mark appears once per
  thumbnail, in a corner. Two R marks read as a typo.
- [ ] **One product, not two.** A thumbnail showing the cards and
  the log and the ebook and the audio pack reads as "too much."
  Pick one. The bundle is the only listing that shows the full
  set in the thumbnail.
- [ ] **One sentence of type, max.** The thumbnail can carry one
  short line of text — the product name, the price anchor, or
  the key feature. Not all three. Type below 36 px does not
  read at thumbnail size.
- [ ] **The 5 colors only.** Cosmic black, electric blue, cyan,
  warm off-white, soft gray. No red, no green, no orange, no
  gold accents. The brand palette is the brand.
- [ ] **No mockup screenshots.** A Canva mockup of an iPhone
  playing a session is a stock-image move. A real flat-lay
  photograph (or a hand-drawn illustration in the brand
  palette) is a Rulio move.
- [ ] **The R mark is not stretched.** The R is a square. Never
  scale it non-uniformly. If the design needs a non-square
  mark, use the favicon
  (`rulio-favicon-256.png`) as a starting point and design
  within that square, not against it.

After Image 1 passes this checklist, design images 2–5 with the
same palette and the same rule of "one product, one message per
image." Image 5 is always the trust image — the founder card, the
disclaimer, the system diagram, or the cheat-sheet preview. A
listing without a trust image converts 20–30% lower than a listing
with one.

## SEO strategy — the why behind every word

Etsy search is a relevance + recency + quality engine. The
relevance score is the sum of how well the title, the tags, the
category, and the attributes match the buyer's query. The recency
boost decays over 30 days, which is why re-listing or renewing a
listing every 3 weeks is a legitimate (and approved) tactic. The
quality score is the click-through rate (CTR) on the listing
thumbnail plus the conversion rate (orders per visit) once the
buyer lands on the listing page.

### Title keyword stack

Each title in this file stacks the keywords in order of buyer
intent:

1. **The product type.** "Solfeggio frequency cards," "Qi
   practice log," "solfeggio audio pack," "rulio bundle." The
   buyer types these words; the title has to start with them.
2. **The format qualifier.** "Printable," "PDF," "MP3," "ebook."
   The buyer filters for format before they filter for brand.
3. **The use case.** "Focus, sleep, energy," "deep work." The
   buyer is buying an outcome, not a feature.
4. **The size or scope.** "A5 + US Letter," "110 minutes," "38-
   page." The buyer is buying enough, not too little.
5. **The brand.** "Rulio Studio." Closes the title and lets
   brand-aware buyers find the shop directly.

This is why each title in this file ends with "Rulio Studio." The
brand name is the only word in the title that does not change
across listings — it is the anchor.

### Tag mix

Every tag list is a mix of three kinds of terms:

1. **High-volume head terms** (3–4 per listing). "Solfeggio
   frequencies," "528 hz," "meditation mp3." These bring
   browsing traffic but compete with thousands of other
   listings.
2. **Long-tail niche terms** (5–6 per listing). "9 solfeggio
   frequencies," "ruler studio," "founder wellness bundle."
   These convert at higher rates because the buyer knows
   exactly what they want.
3. **Use-case terms** (3–4 per listing). "Focus mp3," "sleep
   printable," "deep work audio." The buyer is shopping by
   outcome, not by category.

Avoid single-word tags. Etsy treats single-word tags as broad
match and de-prioritizes them in favor of multi-word phrases.
Avoid competitor brand names. Avoid tags that are already in
the title — Etsy deduplicates them anyway.

### Category and attributes

Each listing has a primary category and one or two attributes
that surface the listing in the left-hand filters.

- **Cards** → Paper & Party Supplies → Paper → Printables
  → Wellness. Attributes: "Instant Download," "Printable,"
  "PDF."
- **Log** → Paper & Party Supplies → Paper → Printables →
  Planners. Attributes: "Instant Download," "Printable,"
  "Daily."
- **Ebook** → Books, Movies & Music → Books → eBooks →
  Self-Help. Attributes: "Instant Download," "PDF," "eBook."
- **Audio pack** → Digital Downloads → Audio & Sounds →
  Meditation. Attributes: "Instant Download," "MP3," "5 min."
- **Bundle** → Digital Downloads → Bundles. Attributes:
  "Instant Download," "PDF + MP3," "Best Value."

The category tree is deep on Etsy; the deeper you go, the less
competition there is. Every listing in this file is filed in
the deepest relevant sub-category for that reason.

### Recency tactic

Every 21 days, click "Renew" on the bundle listing. Etsy's
renewal is free once per listing per 21 days. Renewing
re-indexes the listing in the search results and pushes it
back to the top of the shop grid. The renew is a legitimate
signal that the listing is still active; it is not gaming
the system.

## Competitor positioning

The Etsy solfeggio space has three kinds of sellers. Knowing
where Rulio sits among them is the difference between a listing
that gets buried and a listing that converts.

### The 3 seller archetypes

1. **The dropshipper.** Resells low-resolution MP3s from a
   single stock track. The cover is a stock photo of crystals
   or a wave. The description is 30 words of SEO keyword
   stuffing. The audio is mono, not binaural. The price is
   $3–5.
2. **The healer.** Sells a meditation album with a spiritual
   framing. The cover is a watercolor of a mandala or a
   chakra. The description is full of attributions like "528
   Hz repairs your DNA." The audio is a single 30-minute
   track with the frequency buried under a generic meditation
   music bed. The price is $15–25.
3. **The maker.** Sells a small set of printable cards or
   worksheets. The cover is a flat-lay photograph. The
   description is functional, honest, and short. The audio
   is one-off or absent. The price is $5–12.

### Where Rulio sits

Rulio is a fourth archetype, not in the list above:

4. **The founder-led shop.** Sells a system of related
   products (cards + log + ebook + audio) built by a single
   person with a real product (the Rulio Engine app) behind
   the digital line. The cover is a dark, brand-consistent
   design. The description is honest, evidence-led, and
   short. The audio is a 14-session set generated from a
   documented Python DSP script with a published manifest
   file. The bundle saves 40% off the sum of the parts. The
   price is $7–$49 depending on the listing.

Rulio is not competing with the dropshippers on price (Rulio is
3–10× more expensive). Rulio is not competing with the healers
on attribution (Rulio does not claim the frequencies heal
anything). Rulio is competing for the buyer who has tried the
dropshipper and the healer and felt nothing. That buyer wants
evidence, a system, and a founder they can email. That buyer is
Rulio's customer.

### The differentiation in 1 sentence

> "I tried the generic apps and the YouTube frequency videos
> and felt nothing. The Rulio bundle is the first thing I
> bought that actually changed my day — because the founder
> has the receipts, not the marketing copy." — likely Rulio
> buyer, paraphrased.

This sentence is not a testimonial (it is fabricated and should
not be quoted as one). It is the buyer's voice, used to set
the shop's positioning in the founder's head. Every listing
description, every image, every welcome email should be written
to that buyer.

### What NOT to copy from competitors

- **Crystal-themed cover art.** It reads as dropshipper, not
  founder.
- **The word "healing" anywhere on the listing.** Etsy
  policies prohibit unverified health claims; the word
  triggers a manual review and can lead to a 24-hour hold.
- **Single 60-minute audio tracks.** Binaural beats work in
  short, specific doses. A 60-minute track is a meditation
  track with a frequency pasted on top. The Rulio sessions
  are 5 or 25 minutes by design.
- **Stock photography of white women with crystals.** The
  Rulio shop is a Brussels-based founder, not a crystal
  shop. The imagery should match the brand: dark, electric
  blue, founder-led.

### The 4 listing thumbnails that win the click

The Etsy solfeggio search results are 90% crystal photos and
10% dark, brand-consistent designs. The dark designs win the
click because they are different. Rulio's thumbnails are dark
by default. That is the unfair advantage — Rulio looks like a
product, not a vibe.

## Common Etsy digital-product mistakes — and the fix

These are the mistakes that show up in 80% of first-time Etsy
digital-product shops. Each one is named, explained, and fixed.

### Mistake 1 — single 200 MB ZIP

The Etsy file upload limit is 20 MB per file. A 165 MB audio
bundle does not fit. **Fix:** split the ZIP into 2 parts as
described in the "File delivery on Etsy" section above.

### Mistake 2 — generic thumbnails

Etsy buyers do not click on stock-photo thumbnails. They click
on thumbnails that look like the product. **Fix:** every
thumbnail in this file is a flat-lay of the actual product on
a dark surface, not a stock photo. Image 1 of every listing is
the thumbnail; it must read at 200 × 150 px.

### Mistake 3 — missing the disclaimer

A wellness product on Etsy without a medical disclaimer is
a 24-hour suspension waiting to happen. Etsy's "Handmade &
Vintage" policy requires a clear statement of what the
product is and is not. **Fix:** the disclaimer is in every
description, in the shop About section, and in the shop
policies.

### Mistake 4 — over-tagging

Thirteen tags is the limit. Using all 13 is a feature, not a
bug. Single-word tags like "frequency" or "wellness" waste
the slot. **Fix:** every tag in this file is a 2–4 word
phrase that matches a real search query.

### Mistake 5 — running Ads on every listing at once

Etsy Ads on 5 listings at $5/day = $25/day, which is a
$750/month commitment before you know which listing
converts. **Fix:** Ads on the bundle only at $5/day for
the first 30 days. Expand to the other 4 listings only if
the bundle Ads are profitable.

### Mistake 6 — letting the welcome email go cold

Every Etsy order comes with an automatic "Thanks for your
order" email from Etsy. The shop owner can replace it with
a custom message. **Fix:** in the shop settings, set the
custom message to:

> Thanks for the order. The download link is in your
> Etsy receipt. If it fails, write to hello@rulio.app and
> a human (Roel) will reply within 48 hours. The audio is
> headphones-only — the binaural-beat effect collapses on
> speakers. — Roel

That single email has a 40% open rate (Etsy receipts are
opened) and prevents the most common support ticket (the
buyer plays the audio on speakers and hears "nothing").

### Mistake 7 — forgetting to renew

A listing that has not been renewed in 30 days drops in
search rank. **Fix:** add a recurring 21-day reminder to
renew the bundle listing. The reminder goes on the
founder's calendar, not on a Zapier workflow — the click
takes 2 seconds and is a legitimate recency signal.

## Cross-promotion — the 5 listings as a system

The 5 listings are designed to cross-sell. The descriptions
name the other products by name. The image specs call for
system shots that show the products together. The
verification checklist reminds the founder to link every
listing to the other 4 in the "Related items" section.

The cross-sell funnel inside the Etsy shop runs like this:

1. Buyer lands on the **bundle** listing (highest-intent, Ads
   running).
2. Buyer reads the bundle description, sees the price
   comparison table, sees the "you are buying the founder's
   desk" line.
3. Buyer sees the related items — cards, log, ebook, audio
   pack — and clicks through to one of them.
4. The individual product's description names the bundle as
   the "system" version. The buyer goes back to the bundle.
5. The bundle listing's image 4 has the price comparison.
   The math closes the sale.

This is why image 4 of the bundle listing is the price
comparison table, and image 5 is the founder card. The math
image closes the price-objection buyer. The founder card
closes the trust-objection buyer. Both are in the bundle
listing because the bundle is where the Ads are running.

The individual listings do not need a price comparison image
— they are the comparison. The bundle is the only listing
that needs to do the math, because the bundle is the only
listing that saves money.

## What to monitor — the 5 numbers

After the shop is live, monitor 5 numbers weekly.

1. **Orders per listing.** Which of the 5 listings is selling,
   and which is not. If a listing has fewer than 1 order per
   week after 4 weeks, the thumbnail is the problem. Replace
   image 1.
2. **Etsy Ads CTR.** Target: 1–3%. Below 1%, the thumbnail
   is the problem. Above 3%, the listing is over-priced or
   the Ads budget is too low.
3. **Etsy Ads cost per order.** Target: under $5 on the
   bundle. Above $10, pause the Ads and re-list the bundle
   with a fresh thumbnail.
4. **Conversion rate on the bundle listing.** Target: 2–5%
   of visits become orders. Below 1%, the price is the
   problem. Above 5%, the price is too low.
5. **Refund rate.** Target: under 2%. Above 5%, the product
   is not delivering the description. The most common
   refund reason for digital products is "did not match
   the description" — re-read the description against the
   product to confirm.

These 5 numbers are the dashboard. Everything else is noise.

# Page 4 — Find your worst hour

## Three questions. One frequency.

### 1. What hour of the day are you worst at?

Not your worst day. Your worst **hour**. The one that repeats. Mine is
3 PM. A lot of founders will say the same.

### 2. What does "worst" look like?

Pick one word — or write your own:

- Tired · Anxious · Scattered · Stuck · Numb · Frustrated · Overwhelmed

### 3. What frequency matches it?

Look back at the cheat sheet on page 3. Match the hour + the feeling to
one frequency. One only. Don't pick three. If two could apply, pick the
one that matched the *feeling*, not the *hour*.

# Page 5 — The 5-minute protocol

## The whole protocol, on one page

1. **Headphones on.** Stereo, not mono. The binaural effect needs L/R
   separation. AirPods count. Over-ear counts. One-ear does not.
2. **Pick the frequency.** Whatever you decided on page 4.
3. **Press play.** 5 minutes. Set a timer if you want, but you don't
   have to. The session ends on its own.
4. **Eyes closed or soft focus.** Not staring at a screen. The point
   is to take the 5 minutes.
5. **Breathe normally.** No special technique. No mantra. No
   affirmation. Just breathe.
6. **5 minutes. Done.**

---

## Day 6

Pick a **different** frequency. Same hour. Same 5 minutes. Notice the
difference. If the first one did something, the second will do something
*else* — that comparison is the point. If the first one did nothing,
the second is the test. If nothing happens on day 6 either, you have
your answer: this tool is not for you. That's a useful answer.

---

## A short note on headphones

**Stereo required.** The 9 solfeggio frequencies are delivered as
binaural beats — slightly different tones in the left and right ear.
The effect happens in the brainstem, not in the audio file. Mono
playback (a single speaker, a Bluetooth speaker on the table, one
earbud in) cancels the effect. Use any working pair of stereo
headphones. Volume low. The point is to feel the tone, not to push
through it.

easy to sit with for 5 minutes. That's the whole claim.

hour, not a substitute for a doctor, a therapist, or a friend.

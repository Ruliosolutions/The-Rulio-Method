## How to use this (the 7-step workflow)

1. **Find 1 real person in each archetype.** Spend 5 minutes per archetype on X search, LinkedIn, Substack Recommendations, podcast guest lists, YouTube. Look for someone with the audience size in the profile and who has posted recently on one of the listed topics.
2. **Personalize the placeholder text.** Use the **Personalization Checklist** below — swap each `[bracketed]` item for a real value. Body of the pitch stays the same.
3. **Send in batches of 3-5 per day.** Mix channels (2 emails + 2 DMs + 1 LinkedIn) to avoid spam-flagging and learn what works.
4. **Track in a spreadsheet.** Use the template at the bottom. Without a sheet, attribution evaporates in week 2.
5. **After 5 days of silence, send the follow-up** listed in each partner section. Keep it short.
6. **If they say yes:** reply within 1 hour with the affiliate link and the Pro code. Onboarding speed is the #1 reason affiliates drop off.
7. **If they say no:** move on. Don't argue. The next email is always a better use of your afternoon.

**Total time:** find 1 person (5 min) + personalize (2 min) + send (1 min) = 8 min/partner. 10 partners = 80 minutes. Worth doing on a single Tuesday morning.

## Personalization Checklist (use this for every partner)

Before you send, swap these placeholders. Most are 30 seconds of work each:

- `[first name]` → their actual first name (use "Hi [first name]," not "Dear [first name]")
- `[name]` → handle-style shorthand for Twitter DMs
- `[recent post title]` → the actual title of their most recent relevant post (re-read the post for 30 seconds before referencing it)
- `[recent post / Reel / video]` → swap to match the medium (Reel for IG, video for YouTube, post for Substack)
- `[specific video]` → a specific video title when the pitch mentions one
- `[recent piece / book excerpt]` → swap based on the partner's medium
- `[city]` → if you know the city the partner is based in (sound bath teachers especially)

**Personalize ONE specific thing** beyond the placeholders. The cheapest credibility-builder: quote a single line from their recent work back to them. Example: "Your line 'the afternoon dip is a breath pattern, not a caffeine problem' is the framing I built Rulio around." That single sentence lifts reply rate by 2-3x.

## What NOT to do (5 mistakes that kill reply rates)

1. **Don't send the same pitch to all 10 partners in one day.** Spam-flagging and visible pattern-matching. Batch by channel, not by archetype.
2. **Don't CC multiple partners on the same email.** It has happened. It is funny in retrospect. It is fatal in the moment.
3. **Don't pitch a podcast appearance, a backlink, a co-marketing webinar, or a meeting.** The pitch is the app and the affiliate program. Anything else is a tell that you don't respect their time.
4. **Don't argue when they say no.** "No" is data, not a debate prompt. Move to the next archetype.
5. **Don't send the follow-up from a different email address.** Use the same thread, same address, same sign-off. Consistency reads as honest.

## Tone Calibration (good vs. bad)

**Bad pitch (generic, 240 words, no specificity):**
> "Hi there! I came across your awesome channel and I think you'd be a great fit for Rulio. Rulio is a revolutionary audio app that uses solfeggio frequencies to help people focus, sleep, and have more energy. We have a generous affiliate program and would love to partner with you. Let me know if you're interested! Best, Roel"

Why it fails: zero specificity, no recent-post reference, "revolutionary" buzzword, no commission numbers, no Pro code offered, no clear CTA, no reason to reply.

**Good pitch (this file's template, 170 words, specific):**
> "Hi [first name], your post on [recent post title] hit a nerve — I've had the same 'what's actually playing while I ship' conversation with about 200 founders this quarter. I built Rulio after cycling through every focus app and getting annoyed none were adaptive. 528 for deep work, 396 for reset, 285 for the afternoon crash. 5-min sessions, web app. Two questions: 1) free Pro seat for a real review? 2) 30% recurring for the lifetime of any subscriber you refer. If neither, no hard feelings. — Roel"

Why it works: names a specific post, gives the founder a specific story, explains the product in one line with concrete frequencies, offers a specific deal, has a clean two-CTA close.

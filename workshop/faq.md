# Energy Reset Workshop — FAQ

> **10 questions, founder-led answers.** Copy-paste this onto
> the workshop landing page and into the confirmation email.

---

**Q: I'm not a founder. Is this workshop still for me?**

A: Yes. The workshop is for anyone with a daily state pattern
they want to shift — sleep, focus, anxiety, creative block.
"Founder" is the lead persona because founders are the most
likely to have a state-shifting problem (irregular sleep,
hardest-hour-of-the-day, 3pm dip), but the protocol works
identically for an academic, a writer, a new parent, or a
mid-career operator. The only requirement: you have a daily
state you want to change, and you're willing to run a 5-min
protocol 5× a day for 7 days.

---

**Q: I've read The Rulio Qi Method. Is the workshop redundant?**

A: No. The book gives you the 9 frequencies, the brainwave
bands, and the 12 sessions. The workshop gives you a *custom*
protocol, designed live, in 90 min, with me on the whiteboard.
The book is the theory; the workshop is the implementation.
Book readers get €18 off (€29 vs €47) — bring your Gumroad
receipt to the form.

**Q: I did the free Energy Audit already. Should I still come?**

A: Yes, if you want the group format and the 7-day practice
log. The audit is 30 min, 1-on-1, focused on your protocol.
The workshop is 90 min, 24-person group, focused on the
protocol + the practice. The two are complementary. Most
audit alumni come to the workshop within 60 days.

---

**Q: Will the workshop help with my specific condition?**

A: No. The frequencies are a relaxation tool, not a medical
device, not a treatment for any condition. If you have a
clinical sleep disorder, anxiety disorder, PTSD, depression,
or any other medical condition, please work with a clinician.
The workshop is for the operator who wants to optimise sleep,
focus, and creative flow — not the person who needs medical
care.

**Q: What if I have to cancel?**

A: Full refund up to 24 hours before the workshop. After that,
your seat is yours — you can transfer it to someone else, or
you get the replay + materials. We don't offer refunds after
the workshop starts because the materials are valuable and
the replay is yours.

**Q: What if I can't make the live session?**

A: The replay is yours for 30 days. But live is significantly
better — the protocol design part (the 25-minute live section
where I prescribe a custom 5-step protocol for each attendee)
is the most useful hour of the workshop, and you can't get
that from a recording. If you have a recurring conflict with
Tuesday 19:00 CET, reply to the confirmation email and we'll
find a one-off solution.

---

**Q: I have a history of seizures / I'm pregnant / I have a pacemaker.**

A: Please consult a clinician before using any binaural-beat
or solfeggio frequency audio. The workshop is a relaxation
tool, not a medical device. The 9 frequencies are widely
considered safe for the general population, but they are not
appropriate for everyone.

**Q: Do I need special equipment?**

A: Headphones. Anything — AirPods, Sony, Bose, Sennheiser,
the cheap ones from the drawer. The binaural-beat effect
needs the L signal in one ear and the R signal in the other.
Speakers will collapse the effect. A pen and a piece of paper
for the live protocol design.

---

**Q: Is this a meditation retreat?**

A: No. This is a workshop. The 90 minutes is structured
(90 sec per frequency, 15 min on the math, 25 min live
protocol design, 20 min on the 7-day practice, 10 min on
the offer). You will work. The output is a written protocol
you can run the same day. If you want a meditation retreat,
go to a meditation retreat.

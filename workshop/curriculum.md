## The one-line pitch

Learn to prescribe the right solfeggio frequency for any state — sleep,
focus, anxiety, creative block, pre-pitch — in under 5 minutes, with a
personal protocol you can run the same day.

1. A **written 5-step Qi Method protocol** customised to your sleep
   pattern, your work hours, and your one hardest state.
2. **9 frequency cards** (PDF) — the 9 solfeggio frequencies, what
   each one does, when to use it, how to layer it.
3. **A daily rhythm template** — your morning, deep-work, post-lunch,
   evening, and pre-sleep sessions, written down, with the exact
   frequencies.
4. **A 7-day practice log** — the thing that makes the difference
   between knowing and doing. Fill it in for a week, ship your
   protocol to your phone, run it for 30 days.
5. **A 20% off code for Rulio Engine Pro** (if you want the
   personalised engine, the 25-min extended sessions, and the
   adaptive AI features).

## The 90-min flow

### 00:00 – 00:05 · Open

- The one thing this workshop is NOT (meditation, breathwork retreat,
  healing, spiritual bypassing)
- The one thing this workshop IS (a working protocol you can run
  the same day)
- The rule: "Don't try. The frequencies do the work."

### 00:05 – 00:15 · The 9 frequencies, in 90 seconds each

- 174 Hz — physical foundation (pain, body)
- 285 Hz — cellular healing (deep sleep, recovery)
- 396 Hz — liberation (grief, fear, morning reset)
- 417 Hz — change (anxiety, transition, focus)
- 528 Hz — transformation (love, sleep, second wind)
- 639 Hz — connection (relationships, listening)
- 741 Hz — intuition (decisions, insight)
- 852 Hz — order (mental review, evening)
- 963 Hz — cosmic (alertness, pre-pitch)
- For each: when to use, what it does, what to layer, what NOT to
  expect.

### 00:15 – 00:30 · The binaural-beat math (in plain language)

- Why headphones (L/R frequency difference, brainstem integration)
- The 4 brainwave bands (delta, theta, alpha, beta) and which
  frequency range targets which
- How to read a session spec (carrier Hz, offset Hz, sub-bass, length)
- The two "off-limits" frequencies (anything below 0.5 Hz or above
  20 Hz — outside the safe range)

### 00:30 – 00:55 · Live protocol design

- Each attendee names: their worst state, their hardest hour of
  the day, and their one non-negotiable sleep window.
- Roel prescribes a 5-step protocol on the whiteboard:
  1. Morning (5 min) — 396 Hz, 4 Hz Δ, theta
  2. Deep work (25 min) — 417+852 Hz, 14 Hz Δ, low beta
  3. Post-lunch reset (5 min) — 417 Hz, 6 Hz Δ, low alpha
  4. Pre-sleep (5 min) — 528 Hz, 0.5 Hz Δ, delta
  5. Wake-up at 3am fallback (5 min) — 285 Hz, 1 Hz Δ, delta
- Q&A: "What if my hardest hour is at 6pm?" — adjust, no dogma.

### 00:55 – 01:15 · The 7-day practice

- The 7-day log template (paper or digital, both supplied)
- What to track: time of day, frequency used, length, what you
  noticed, what didn't work
- Why 7 days is the minimum to feel the difference
- The phone-home: how to use the audio without it becoming
  another productivity system you abandon by Wednesday

### 01:15 – 01:25 · The post-workshop offer

- The 20% off code for Rulio Engine Pro
- The €500 Energy Reset Bundle (workshop + 6-month Engine Pro +
  2 private sessions) for serious operators
- The free Energy Audit (30 min) for anyone who wants to keep
  going after the workshop

### 01:25 – 01:30 · Close

- The one rule: run the protocol for 7 days, then decide.
- The one piece of homework: write down your worst state, your
  hardest hour, your one non-negotiable sleep window, in 3 sentences.
  Bring it to the live design session.
- The next workshop date.

## Who this is for

- **The book reader** who read *The Rulio Qi Method* and wants
  help building their own protocol. €29 with proof of book purchase.
- **The audit alumni** who did the free 30-min call and is ready
  for the group format. €47 standard.
- **The chronic state-shifter** who has tried Calm, Headspace,
  Muse, Apollo, etc. and wants something that works at the
  frequency level, not the app level.

## Who this is NOT for

- Anyone looking for a meditation retreat, breathwork training,
  or spiritual experience. We are not that.
- Anyone with a history of seizures, who is pregnant, or who wears
  a pacemaker. The frequencies are a relaxation tool, not a
  medical device.
- Anyone who wants to be told what to feel. The protocol is
  pragmatic; you are the operator.

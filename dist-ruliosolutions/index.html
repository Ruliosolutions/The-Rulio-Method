FAQ.

The 6 questions that come up most. If yours isn't here, the form below gets to Roel directly.

Is this a wellness product or a tech product?
Tech. The Rulio Engine is a Next.js 14 application with Web Audio API, Tone.js, and an AI coach (state-to-session recommender + custom session generator). The wellness framing is the use case; the engine is the product. White-label deployments ship the full source code with the brand stripped and re-skinned.
What about the medical claims compliance?
Every public surface in the Rulio ecosystem carries the disclaimer: a relaxation tool, not a medical device, not a treatment for any condition. White-label deployments keep that disclaimer. We can reword it for your jurisdiction, but we don't drop it. If your sector (insurance, health) requires different language, we work with your legal team to scope it.
How is the engine different from Calm / Headspace / Apollo?
Calm and Headspace are meditation apps. The content is breathwork, body scan, sleep stories. Rulio is frequency math. The output is a written protocol you can run in 5 minutes, 5 times a day. Apollo is a wearable + frequency app, but it's consumer-grade. The Rulio Engine is the operator-grade version — built for founders, athletes, executives who already know what they need and want a tool that delivers the frequency, not the narrative.
What's the minimum contract?
Team licences: 1 year, 10 seats minimum. White-label: 1 year, €54,000 minimum (€4,500 × 12). Platform integration: 1 year, €144,000 minimum (€12,000 × 12), plus a per-active-user fee. All renewable annually. We don't do month-to-month.
What does "active user" mean for the per-seat fee?
A user who has played at least one session in the trailing 30 days. We bill on MAU, not registered users. This keeps the pricing aligned with actual value delivered — if your team abandons the engine, the fee drops.
Can we build custom session catalogues?
Yes. Up to 50 sessions in the standard white-label package. Each custom session is a solfeggio frequency + carrier + offset + sub-bass + length, plus an instruction script. We build them with your clinical / coaching team, ship them as JSON, and the engine renders them on the next deploy. Beyond 50, we scope it as a custom build.
